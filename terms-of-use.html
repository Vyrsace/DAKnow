Reading historical evidence

Dates, version labels, service status and package associations reflect the evidence available for the particular record. Publication dates, announced patch schedules, observed availability, archive captures and file timestamps can describe different events. Read the date qualifications, Research and Sources before treating a date as a first release or a client as a final build.

Official publisher material, official-hosted player submissions, community research and catalog interpretation have different evidentiary roles. A citation does not turn every statement in the cited source into verified fact. A missing record does not establish that a version never existed. Historical services, links and documented behavior may no longer be available.

Downloads and file verification

The Downloads section includes selected historical packages and external mirrors. Original and Repack identify packaging within this catalog; these labels do not mean publisher endorsement, a new software license or a guarantee of safe execution. A repack can differ from its source container while preserving the described runtime files. Consult each package's notes for missing volumes, required base versions, installation support and other limitations.

  • Download and use software only where you have the necessary legal rights, and follow any applicable publisher license or end-user agreement.
  • A matching SHA-256 establishes correspondence with the exact object described in the record. It does not establish that a file is harmless, signed by a publisher, compatible with your system or permitted for redistribution.
  • Historical clients may depend on obsolete components or unavailable services. A download does not guarantee installation, login, support or current in-game availability.
  • Keep independent backups and evaluate files before opening or executing them. The catalog's preservation and research descriptions are not instructions to bypass access controls or licensing requirements.
