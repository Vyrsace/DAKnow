        [Card.Title]

            어둠의 전설
        [/Card.Title]
        [Company.Name]Nexon Korea[/Company.Name]
        [Website.Label]Website[/Website.Label]
        [Website.URL]https://lod.nexon.com/[/Website.URL]
        [Years]1998 - Present[/Years]
        [Region]South Korea[/Region]
        [Paragraph.1]Developed and published by Nexon Korea, Legends of Darkness entered commercial service in South Korea on January 3, 1998 and remains actively developed through regular gameplay updates, bug fixes and seasonal events.[/Paragraph.1]
        [Paragraph.2]Adventure in Mysosia through five starting classes, with class changes and promotions that allow different skill combinations. Group combat emphasizes complementary roles, while guilds compete for control of towns.[/Paragraph.2]
    [/Card]

    [Card ID="03"]
        [Card.Title]
            Elancia
            일랜시아
        [/Card.Title]
        [Company.Name]Nexon Korea[/Company.Name]
        [Website.Label]Website[/Website.Label]
        [Website.URL]https://elancia.nexon.com/[/Website.URL]
        [Years]1998 - Present[/Years]
        [Region]South Korea[/Region]
        [Paragraph.1]Announced in December 1998 as Nexon’s third online game, Elancia was designed as a shared fantasy society blending Eastern-style artwork with Western mythology. Nexon Korea continues to operate the Korean edition.[/Paragraph.1]
        [Paragraph.2]Abilities provide specialization distinct from ordinary character levels, across warrior, adventurer and merchant roles. Combat sits alongside fishing, cooking, tailoring and jewelry-making, giving players several ways to develop their characters.[/Paragraph.2]
    [/Card]

    [Card ID="04"]
        [Card.Title]
            Asgard
            아스가르드
        [/Card.Title]
        [Company.Name]Nexon Korea[/Company.Name]
        [Website.Label]Website[/Website.Label]
        [Website.URL]https://asgard.nexon.com/[/Website.URL]
        [Years]2001 - Present[/Years]
        [Region]South Korea[/Region]
        [Paragraph.1]Developed by Nexon Korea and originally presented as DarkAges VI – Asgard, this related Windows MMORPG entered public testing in 2001. Commercial service began on February 4, 2003 and remains active in South Korea.[/Paragraph.1]
        [Paragraph.2]Character growth combines four progression tracks: level, alignment, fame and faith. Players choose good or evil as they advance into the celestial realm, raise guardian pets and compete in guild castle sieges.[/Paragraph.2]
    [/Card]

    [Card ID="05"]
        [Card.Title]DarkAges (SGDA)[/Card.Title]
        [Company.Name]NexonAsia[/Company.Name]
        [Website.Label]Website[/Website.Label]
        [Website.URL]https://web.archive.org/web/20020826070815id_/http://darkages.nexonasia.com:80/archive/archive_9_2000.htm[/Website.URL]
        [Years]2000 - 2003[/Years]
        [Region]Singapore[/Region]
        [Paragraph.1]NexonAsia introduced its Singapore DarkAges service in 2000, with beta testing in June and paid service beginning on September 30. It operated alongside Singapore’s later Legends of Darkness service; NexonAsia announced a shutdown for the end of June 2003.[/Paragraph.1]
        [Paragraph.2]Combined cooperative monster hunting and potion brewing with courtship, marriage and an Aisling community. Local events and civic ranks in Suomi and Rucesion formed part of NexonAsia’s service, sharing the social traditions of DarkAges.[/Paragraph.2]
    [/Card]

        [Region]Japan[/Region]
        [Paragraph.1]Nexon Japan began beta testing of Yami, its edition localized into Japanese, on April 16, 2001. Commercial service followed on September 4, 2001 and ended on November 30, 2005.[/Paragraph.1]
        [Paragraph.2]Skill proficiency grew through repeated use, alongside experience-based leveling and later class changes. Guilds could form alliances, declare hostilities and compete for castles in Mysosia, while marriage and lotteries added social activities beyond hunting.[/Paragraph.2]
    [/Card]

    [Card ID="07"]
        [Card.Title]Legends of Darkness (SGLoD)[/Card.Title]
        [Company.Name]NexonAsia[/Company.Name]
        [Website.Label]Website[/Website.Label]
        [Website.URL]https://www.nexon.com/main/en[/Website.URL]
        [Years]2002 - 2003[/Years]
        [Region]Singapore[/Region]
        [Paragraph.1]Introduced by NexonAsia in December 2002, this separate service localized the Korean Legends of Darkness into English. Its beta ran alongside SGDA; NexonAsia announced the regional services’ shutdown for the end of June 2003.[/Paragraph.1]
        [Paragraph.2]A localization of the Korean game into English, sharing its fantasy setting and core character professions. Party combat paired fighters and martial artists with rogues, spellcasters and healers whose abilities supported one another.[/Paragraph.2]
    [/Card]

        [Years]2001 - 2002[/Years]
        [Region]Malaysia[/Region]
        [Paragraph.1]Operated in Malaysia by TIME dotNet in partnership with NexonAsia, Legends of Orphelias entered beta testing in 2001. Its server closed on May 31, 2002 as the service moved to Legends of Temuair.[/Paragraph.1]
        [Paragraph.2]Preserved community accounts describe a reworking of DarkAges with different maps, professions, skills and spells, open combat between players, and creature companions that grew over time.[/Paragraph.2]

        [Region]Malaysia[/Region]
        [Paragraph.1]Available by 2002 through NexonAsia and local operator TIME dotNet in Malaysia as the successor to Legends of Orphelias.[/Paragraph.1]
        [Paragraph.2]Built on the Temuair edition of DarkAges, combining group adventure with trade, crafting, eight interactive religions and player politics. Players could tailor armour, make weapons and take part in contests within the same virtual society.[/Paragraph.2]
