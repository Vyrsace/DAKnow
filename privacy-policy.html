Scope and current site

This policy describes the DarkAges International Catalog maintained by Vyrsace and published through GitHub Pages. It covers the catalog pages and their own browser scripts. It does not govern GitHub accounts, external archives, corporate websites, game services or third-party download mirrors, each of which operates under its own privacy practices.

The current website is a static research site. It has no catalog account system, login form, checkout, newsletter, on-site comments or contact form. You can read its pages without giving the maintainer a name, email address, password or payment details.

Search, filters and browser storage

Search text, filter selections, sorting and image-viewer actions are processed in your browser. The site's scripts do not submit those inputs to the maintainer or an analytics endpoint.

The main catalog uses tab-scoped sessionStorage under the key daknow.sections.v1 to remember whether its six expandable sections are open or closed. The stored values are section identifiers and open/closed settings; they contain no account identity, search history or download history. The catalog does not transmit this setting to a server.

Browser session storage is separate from cookies. It generally lasts for the tab's browsing session, including refreshes; browser session-restoration features may preserve it longer. You can clear it through your browser's site-data controls. If storage is unavailable or disabled, the sections still work with their default state.

Cookies, analytics and page resources

The catalog's own scripts do not set cookies, create tracking identifiers, run advertising analytics or use fingerprinting. They do not use persistent localStorage. No advertising or social-media tracking widgets are embedded.

Images, styles, scripts and the catalog's font subset are supplied from this site's files or embedded data. Displaying the title galleries does not require an automatic image request to the source publisher or wiki. Ordinary browser caching may retain site resources locally according to your browser and the host's settings.

These descriptions apply to the catalog implementation, rather than a promise that its host or an external service collects no data.

GitHub Pages and download hosting

GitHub provides the website hosting. GitHub's Pages documentation states that visitors' IP addresses are logged and stored for security purposes, whether or not they are signed in to GitHub. The maintainer does not control those host logs or their retention.

Selected historical packages are served through GitHub Releases. Following a download link sends a request to GitHub and possibly its delivery infrastructure. GitHub's own handling of service requests and personal data is described in the GitHub General Privacy Statement. Its rules also apply when you sign in to GitHub, open an issue, comment or contribute through the repository.

The catalog does not maintain its own visitor database or collect its own server logs. The absence of catalog analytics does not prevent GitHub from processing the information necessary to host and protect its services.

External links and downloads

The catalog links to publisher websites, corporate pages, archived captures, community sources and download mirrors. When you follow a link, the destination receives the request and may process your IP address, browser information and other data under its own policy. Your browser may also send a referring page address according to its referrer settings.

The maintainer does not control those services' cookies, accounts, download records, redirects or tracking. Read their policies before providing information or downloading files. Merely listing a source URL does not mean that the website automatically loads that external source while you browse the catalog.

Information you choose to submit

Corrections and project questions can be submitted through DAKnow repository issues. GitHub can display your username, message, attachments and contribution history publicly. The maintainer can read information you choose to provide there to assess a correction or respond to a project question.

Include only what is needed to identify the record and support the report. Do not post passwords, account recovery details, private email addresses, addresses, identity documents or private correspondence. Public submissions may be copied, archived or retained in repository history. Removing or editing an issue does not guarantee removal of copies held by others.

For sensitive GitHub account or personal-data concerns, use GitHub Support and its appropriate private reporting process. A public issue is not a confidential contact channel.

Names and information in historical sources

The catalog can include source authors' names or game handles, screenshot nameplates, publication dates and public source links as part of historical attribution. Such information comes from the cited material, rather than an account or profile created for catalog visitors. It is included to identify the work and preserve the research context, not to build visitor profiles.

If a citation or image contains personal information that should not be displayed, identify the affected page and material in a report while avoiding repetition of the sensitive information. Attribution and source context can be reviewed without assuming that all information once published remains appropriate to republish.

Retention, sharing and security

The catalog's section settings remain in your browser's session storage and can be cleared there. Public research records and repository contributions can remain in the catalog and its version history while they serve the documented research purpose. There is no catalog visitor-data account to delete and no catalog-created cross-site advertising profile.

The maintainer does not sell browser settings or visitor search inputs and does not send them to advertising services. Host logs, GitHub contributions and external-service records are handled by those providers under their own policies and retention rules. Information deliberately posted in a public repository is accessible to others.

The published site uses HTTPS, but no website or public reporting channel guarantees absolute security. The catalog cannot erase host logs, third-party archives, forks or independent copies. Request changes from the service that holds the relevant data.

Your choices and privacy questions

You can clear the site's browser storage and cache, disable JavaScript or use your browser's privacy and referrer controls. Disabling JavaScript affects filters, remembered section state and image-viewer controls. External services may offer their own privacy choices.

For a correction to information displayed in the catalog or a question about its own scripts, use DAKnow repository issues without sharing unnecessary personal data. For requests about data held by GitHub, follow the rights and contact information in the GitHub Privacy Statement. Any rights available under applicable privacy law remain unaffected by this policy.

The catalog is not designed to solicit personal information from children. Anyone using external accounts or services should follow the provider's age and account requirements.

Policy changes

This policy will be revised if the catalog's data practices change, including if forms, analytics, remote embeds or additional storage are introduced. The revision date appears at the top of this page. Third-party providers can change their policies independently; their linked policies describe their current practices.
