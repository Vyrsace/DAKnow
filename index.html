Titles

DarkAges

KRU Interactive

darkages.com

1999 - Present

United States

Launched in 1999 by Nexon’s U.S. company under game director Dave Kennerly. The company became independent as KRU Interactive in 2005 and continues to operate DarkAges for an international player community.

A fantasy role-playing world centered on Temuair, where Aislings explore lands of faeries and magic. Adventure grows alongside a society shaped by player-run politics, religion, storytelling and art.

DarkAges (SGDA)

NexonAsia

nexon.com

2000 - 2003

Singapore

NexonAsia introduced its Singapore DarkAges service in 2000, with beta testing in June and paid service beginning on September 30. It operated alongside Singapore’s later Legends of Darkness service; NexonAsia announced a shutdown for the end of June 2003.

An English-language edition of DarkAges set in Temuair, with Aislings, cooperative adventure and a player society shaped by politics, religion and storytelling.

Legends of Darkness어둠의 전설

Nexon Korea

lod.nexon.com

1998 - Present

South Korea

Developed and published by Nexon Korea, Legends of Darkness entered commercial service in South Korea on January 3, 1998 and remains active there.

A fantasy adventure set in Mysosia, where warriors, rogues, wizards, priests and martial artists develop complementary abilities through class changes and advancement.

Legends of Darkness (Singapore)

NexonAsia

nexon.com

2002 - 2003

Singapore

NexonAsia announced this English-language Singapore beta in December 2002. A paid launch was planned for February 2003; NexonAsia announced a shutdown for the end of June 2003 alongside SGDA.

An English-language localization of the Korean Legends of Darkness, with its class-based fantasy adventure and complementary character professions.

Yami闇の伝説

Nexon Japan

jp.nexon.com

2001 - 2005

Japan

Operated by Nexon Japan, this Japanese-language edition began beta testing on April 16, 2001 and entered commercial service on September 4, 2001. Its service ended on November 30, 2005.

A Japanese-language fantasy adventure in Mysosia, a world inspired by medieval Europe. Players choose professions, explore quests and rely on their companions’ different strengths.

Legends of Orphelias

NexonAsia

nexon.com

2001 - 2002

Malaysia

Introduced by NexonAsia with local operator TIME dotNet in Malaysia in 2001, this English-language edition entered beta that year. Its server closed on May 31, 2002 as the service moved to Legends of Temuair.

An English-language fantasy MMORPG with its own lands, adventuring paths, skills and spells. Its mythology follows the mutual destruction of Chadul and Danaan, after which Aisling sparks are born into Orphelias. The game offered extensive player-versus-player combat and growing creature companions.

Legends of Temuair

NexonAsia

nexon.com

2002 - 2004

Malaysia

Available by 2002 through NexonAsia and local operator TIME dotNet in Malaysia as the successor to Legends of Orphelias.

An English-language fantasy world of towns, forests, crypts and labyrinths, inspired by mythic Europe and Asian animation. Cooperation, trade, religion and politics form part of the adventure alongside spells, skills and crafting.

Asgard아스가르드

Nexon Korea

asgard.nexon.com

2001 - Present

South Korea

Developed by Nexon Korea and originally presented as DarkAges VI – Asgard, this related Windows MMORPG entered public testing in 2001. Commercial service began on February 4, 2003 and remains active in South Korea.

A separate fantasy MMORPG set in Mysosia, where character professions, cooperative dungeons and the conflict between good and evil shape the adventure.

真世界 [Zhēn Shìjiè]

APEXON Technology

apexon-online.com.tw (archived)

2001 (Announced)

Taiwan

Licensed from Nexon and localized by APEXON Technology (捷生資訊), 真世界 was promoted in Taiwan in 2001. The original site documents recruitment for selected-player Chinese client testing; a full commercial launch remains unconfirmed.

A Traditional Chinese edition of DarkAges set in Temuair, with its mythology, adventuring professions and a player community built around towns, trade and shared adventure.

Verification Notes

File and package identity

SHA-256 identifies the original installer or patch file and appears below its download filename. Each link names the file it actually opens. For archive and disc-image downloads, the installer named after “Contents:” is the one verified by the hash; the hash does not identify the containing package.

Mirror labels distinguish download locations. Identical original files share one verified identity even when their linked filenames differ. Repacked archives identify installed or extracted files; multipart volumes appear together and omit individual hashes. An absent hash leaves the original standalone installer identity unspecified.

Package comparisons include every extracted file: executables, data, text, configuration, music and supporting files. Matching one executable or a version label alone does not establish whole-client equality. Extraction paths and packaging comparisons appear in Research Notes.

Regional variants

The Singapore 3.51 distributions differ in embedded help text. Temuair and Orphelias-labeled 4.20 packages share core files but differ in configuration and music.

No complete client packages have been established as identical across confirmed country editions. Individual files can match across releases: Singapore and U.S. 3.51 share a DarkAges.dat variant; Korean 9.100 production and 9.570 Hades share Legend.dat.
